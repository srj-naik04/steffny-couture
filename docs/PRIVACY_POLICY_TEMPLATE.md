## What we collect

When you use the App or Site, we collect:

**Information you provide:**
- Name, phone number, and email address (when booking an alteration or creating an account)
- Photos of garments you wish to have altered
- Notes and details about your alteration requirements
- Appointment date and time preferences

**Information collected automatically:**
- Device type and operating system
- App version
- Push notification token (if you grant permission)
- Crash reports and diagnostic data (if you opt in)

We do not collect:
- Location data
- Payment information (payments are handled in-person at the studio)
- Advertising identifiers
- Data for tracking across apps or websites

## How we use it

We use your information to:
- Process and manage your alteration bookings
- Contact you about your bookings (confirmations, status updates, ready-for-collection notifications)
- Provide customer service and respond to enquiries
- Improve the App's functionality and resolve issues
- Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third parties for marketing.

## Who has access

Within Steffny Couture Ltd: Steffi Da Cruz and authorised staff with a need to fulfil your booking.

Third-party processors:
- **Supabase** (database, file storage, authentication) — hosted in the European Union
- **Expo** (push notification delivery)
- **[SMTP provider]** (email delivery)

These processors are bound by contractual data protection obligations.

## How long we keep it

- Booking records: 6 years (UK statutory requirement for business records)
- Photos: 12 months after the booking is completed, then deleted unless you request earlier deletion
- Account data: until you request deletion

## Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
- Object to processing
- Data portability
- Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk)

To exercise any of these, contact us at [contact email]. We will respond within one calendar month.

## Children

The App is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us data, please contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

We use industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. No system is 100% secure; in the event of a personal data breach, we will notify affected users and the ICO as required by UK GDPR.

## Cookies

The Site uses essential cookies only (for session management and basic functionality). We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. The App does not use cookies.

## Changes to this policy

We will notify users of material changes via in-app notice or email. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent version.
